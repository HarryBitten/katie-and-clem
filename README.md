# Katie & Clem

A little pixel-art browser game: walk Katie around the world, one life stage at a time.

Play it: https://harrybitten.github.io/katie-and-clem/

- Hold the right arrow (or D) to walk. M turns the music on and off.
- No build step and no image or sound files. Everything is drawn and played in code.

## Files
- `index.html` – the page
- `js/font.js` – the pixel font
- `js/gfx.js` – people, bikes and Stanley
- `js/scenery.js` – buildings, landmarks and props
- `js/game.js` – the world, the story text and the game loop
